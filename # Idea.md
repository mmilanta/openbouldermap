# Idea

I am thinking of a videogame that resembles the emotions of looking at a night sky with a telescope.

I am not sure about the gameplay yet. For a v0 I would like the possibility to look at a night sky. and zoom into some features. Features should be:
* stars
* sun
* moons
* planets

The idea is that we can see with time passing (with a time dial how stuff moves).

The idea is that the rendering engine should be general, and in the future we would like to be able to setup multiple solar systems. For now keep it one.
